Q: Why Orion as a name? What's the signifigance?

Orion has 3 roots, First, Orion is one of our favorite constellations in the sky, second, the greek origin of the constellation is a mythical hunter, third, 'orion' is very close to another greek work 'horion' which means something like 'the edge' or 'horizon'. The combination of these ideas for us means: Orion is the search for the horizon. The search for the limit of the sky and space. It is about the journey to reach for the stars, together.
